# Market workflow scope

Official source links and market-specific document guidance are maintained in `shared/markets.ts`, reviewed 14 October 2026. The workflow measures preparation progress, not legal eligibility or probability of listing. Issuer domicile, segment, security type, offering route, local venue and current rules require qualified adviser review.

US offerings may use S-1 or F-1 depending on issuer status. B3 uses applicable CVM/B3 documentation. LSE distinguishes Main Market and AIM under the current UK regime. Euronext requires local venue and segment. HKEX distinguishes boards and specialized chapters. Tokyo distinguishes Prime, Standard and Growth. Other produces only a general preparation inventory.

The 24 starter criteria are explicit, equal-weight preparation tasks across six categories. They are not a comprehensive set of statutory listing requirements. Missing or unreviewed evidence receives no completion credit. No automatic N/A exclusions or model-generated percentages are supported.
